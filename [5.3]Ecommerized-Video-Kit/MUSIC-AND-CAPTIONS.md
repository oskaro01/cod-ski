# Music suggestions, captions & CTAs

The three MP4s ship with **burned-in captions** and a **silent stereo audio track**.
The silent track is deliberate: music is licensed at publish time, not baked into a
master. Drop your chosen bed onto the existing audio track and the file is ready.

Matching `.srt` sidecars are in `captions/` for platforms that prefer uploaded
subtitles (YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn) or for translation.

## 1. Music suggestions

I can't ship licensed audio, so this is a **brief you can act on** rather than a file.
Every video's scene cuts are listed below so you can land a drop on a cut.

### Where to source (all cleared for commercial/ad use)

| Source | Why | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok Commercial Music Library** | Pre-cleared for business accounts and Spark Ads. Use this if the video runs as a TikTok ad. | Free with a Business account |
| **Meta Sound Collection** | Same idea for Facebook/Instagram ads. Cleared inside Meta's ecosystem. | Free |
| **Uppbeat** | Good modern-tech catalogue, clear licence terms, free tier with credit. | Free / ~$7 mo |
| **Artlist** or **Epidemic Sound** | Best quality and the safest paper trail for paid media. | ~$15–25 mo |

> If the videos run as **paid ads**, use the platform's own library (TikTok CML / Meta
> Sound Collection) or a subscription licence. A "free for YouTube" track is not
> automatically cleared for paid social — that distinction gets accounts struck.

### Per-video brief

**1 · Product showcase (18s)**
- **Mood:** clean, forward-moving, optimistic tech. Confident, not hypey.
- **Search terms:** `minimal tech`, `corporate innovation`, `clean electronic`, `product reveal`
- **BPM:** 110–120 · **Key feel:** major
- **Structure:** soft pulse under the hook, add a light arp at the dashboard reveal, full
  bed from the module grid to the end.
- **Land the lift at 14.2s** (module grid pops in) so the chips pop on the beat.

**2 · Unboxing / review (24s)**
- **Mood:** conversational and warm — this reads as a real person's review, so the music
  sits *under* it rather than driving it. Light percussion, no big drop.
- **Search terms:** `chill lofi beat`, `warm ukulele tech`, `light indie pop`, `vlog background`
- **BPM:** 90–100 · Keep it **quiet (-18 to -22 LUFS under the captions)**.
- **Structure:** minimal in the hook, drop the low end during the "old stack" section for
  a slight tension, bring warmth back at the unboxing.
- **Turn at 8.0s** (old stack → new platform) and **12.2s** (scorecard).

**3 · Promotional ad (15s)**
- **Mood:** punchy, urgent, high contrast. This is a direct-response ad — the music does
  the pacing work.
- **Search terms:** `energetic trap ad`, `punchy corporate`, `hard hitting promo`, `driving electronic`
- **BPM:** 128–140 · Needs a clear **impact hit at 6.6s**, when "$427" flips to "$49".
- **Structure:** tense/sparse for the problem (0–6.6s), drop on the switch (6.6s), full
  energy through proof and CTA.
- Add a subtle **whoosh** on each scene cut and a **UI click** as each module chip pops.

### Mixing

- Duck the music if you add a voiceover; otherwise keep the bed at **-16 LUFS** integrated.
- Most feeds autoplay **muted** — everything essential is already carried by the burned-in
  captions and on-screen type. The music is enhancement, never load-bearing.
- Keep the last 0.5s clean so the loop restart doesn't clip.
