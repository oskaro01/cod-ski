# Marketplace, UGC and customer-demand evidence

## Objective and inputs

Investigate observable demand for one candidate's solution and customer problem, while keeping exact-product evidence distinct. Use Product ID, original/current direct product URLs, exact variant, target GCC countries and prior marketplace/customer/creator records. Reuse available research and focus on a stated evidence gap; if none is specified, investigate the largest unresolved demand question. The legacy filename is retained; search-interest/keyword analysis is excluded.

This standalone tool supplies evidence to the workflow. It does not change Individual Prompt 2, override screening gates or approve a sample.

In staged Individual Prompt 2, this is the P2-3 demand/UGC module. Reuse P2-2 marketplace findings and the current source register; research only missing or conflicting evidence, not a second complete market study. Return this module's evidence findings, demand assessment and checkpoint under the controller's stage budget. Creative proposals required by the main prompt may be appended compactly in that stage. Pause after P2-3; no final commercial approval or exports here. Standalone use below remains supported.

## Four evidence layers

| Layer | What to investigate | What it can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Exact product | Exact brand, model and variant | Traction for that identified product |
| Direct competitor | Comparable solution with a similar function | Demand for a competing solution, with differences disclosed |
| Category | Relevant category and alternative names | Interest in the broader solution class |
| Customer problem | Problem, routine, use case and desired benefit | Interest in the problem; purchase intent needs separate evidence |

Explain comparability. A sterilizer/dryer is not automatically equivalent to a bottle-washing machine; features, function and buying intent must match the claim. Category and problem evidence may support exploration but cannot satisfy an exact-product verification requirement.

Candidate advancement needs at least one qualifying buying signal for the exact product or an identified direct comparable with justified functional/price relevance. Category/problem-only findings remain broader interest; candidate buying evidence is UNKNOWN. Comparable-only evidence supports a solution opportunity, not proven sales of the candidate's exact model.

## Focused research

1. Use up to four new focused English/Arabic demand source-finding queries in total per product across the four layers and GCC countries, starting with up to two accessible source types overall. These are first-pass limits, not per-country quotas. Default launch country is UAE; follow useful GCC evidence without six compulsory country studies. Reuse earlier source records; when supporting screening, its tighter source/output limits govern. Do not imply unresearched countries were assessed.
2. Do not use Google Trends, Keyword Planner, keyword volume, autocomplete, CPC or search visibility as demand inputs. Ordinary web search only locates evidence; results/rank do not earn demand points. A second pass needs a named conflict or decision-changing gap. After access failure, try an accessible alternative then record the limitation. Capture checked date and source snapshot/measurement period; cached evidence is not automatically current.
3. Read supplied marketplace/order/customer screenshots only for visible product, date, country, variant, counts and measurement period. Mark hidden fields NOT CAPTURED. Screenshots are supplied evidence, not independently retrieved records. Do not invent source URLs or compare incompatible periods/variants.
4. Search useful creator platforms for exact product, competitors, category and problem demonstrations. Prefer up to six strong new posts overall; stop earlier when sufficient, and do not fill a quota with weak content. Reuse relevant existing references. Add more only for a named unresolved decision-critical question in a later run.
5. Open direct video destinations. Record creator/account, platform, publication date, visible views/likes/comments/shares, sponsorship status, hook, demonstration and evidence layer. Metadata-only/empty-player responses are NOT INSPECTED for demonstration claims. Label original hands-on articles EDITORIAL HANDS-ON and manufacturer footage BRAND DEMO; neither becomes an independent creator video. Mark unavailable metrics NOT CAPTURED and inaccessible links NOT VERIFIED. Keep negative/safety content under risks. Mark posts older than 12 months HISTORICAL.
6. Keep GCC and international evidence separate. Creator location does not establish audience location. Record the basis of each geographical claim; global content can support creative/category potential without establishing GCC traction.
7. Follow this buying-evidence sequence within the same budget: inspect relevant marketplace/retailer bought/sold indicators, then readable product reviews, then independent buyer discussions only for a material corroboration gap. Capture exact displayed metric/wording, model/variant, country basis, measurement period and rounding; never turn lower bounds into exact sales. Rankings, prices, availability and headline stars alone are context, not purchasing evidence. Reviews proxy buying, not unit sales. Keep product feedback distinct from seller/store-wide/global pools. Reuse adequate checks instead of repeating them.

For each accessible relevant listing, inspect up to five recent and five low-rated written reviews, deduplicate overlaps and record actual counts, dates, model, purchase/use context and purchase-status basis. Inspect fewer if fewer are readable; zero needs a reason and a recovery action when material. This selected sample does not estimate population return/defect rates. For a buyer discussion, capture the original URL, author/date, country basis, model and actual purchase/use statement. Recommendations and intentions are not purchases; no discussion quota applies.

Use the existing source register for buying evidence, not a second ledger. Add evidence type, briefly transcribed observed wording/metric, buyer-geography basis, purchase-status basis and independence group/reason. Purchase labels are PLATFORM-REPORTED PURCHASE, SELF-REPORTED PURCHASE, OWNERSHIP ONLY, GIFT OR SAMPLE or UNKNOWN; they describe source support, not guaranteed authenticity. Ownership, gifts and samples may inform quality/creative assessment but do not alone establish paid buying activity. Keep buying activity, satisfaction/quality and creative popularity separate.

If only private evidence can resolve a decision-changing gap, record a redacted merchant-record request with owner/timing as EXTERNAL INPUT NEEDED; do not contact anyone automatically. Specify SKU, country, reporting period, units versus orders, returns/cancellations basis and wholesale shipments versus end-customer sales. Supplier assertions remain SUPPLIER-REPORTED, not independent consumer-demand proof. Do not make private records mandatory when public evidence already supports the decision.

Calculate engagement only if views are positive and likes, comments and shares are all captured: `(likes + comments + shares) / views * 100`. Different platform metrics are not automatically comparable.

Independence concerns the underlying merchant dataset or customer population, not URL/site/screenshot counts. Assign stable per-product independence group IDs with a short reason; uncertain independence cannot count as proven additional corroboration. Shared review pools, syndicated merchant records/data feeds, reposts and the same buyer count once. A badge and reviews on one listing are not automatically independent; justify any claimed separate datasets/populations.

Prefer buyer reviews and purchasing discussions from the preceding 12 months relative to the research date. Older evidence is HISTORICAL; justify any longer buying-cycle window explicitly. Review dates are feedback dates, not exact purchase dates. Preserve historical safety/quality concerns. Sales badges need a checked snapshot and their exact displayed measurement period; undisclosed dates/periods are NOT CAPTURED, not assumed recent. Prices/stock need the actual check date and any cached-source date; retrieving old evidence today does not refresh it.

## Assessment and output

Maximum 900 words including tables, excluding URL strings. Return only:

### A. Evidence findings

| Evidence ID / direct source or screenshot | Layer / product match | Country / buyer-geography basis | Date / period | Observed claim / metric / purchase-status basis | Inspection / independence group and reason / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use up to ten strongest evidence rows total. Preserve any additional completed source records needed for the pending question in the checkpoint. Do not manufacture links for screenshots or inaccessible videos.

### B. Demand interpretation

State the best-supported exact-product/comparable buying signals, country scope, recency, independent source count, objections and limitations. Apply Demand Index v3.0, purchasing evidence only: UNKNOWN when buying activity cannot be assessed or listings/stock/prices are the only observations; 0 only for affirmative negligible-current-activity evidence; 25 weak/isolated/stale purchasing clues; 50 relevant current GCC buying/feedback evidence with limited corroboration; 75 at least two independent relevant GCC signals including recent purchasing/credible buyer feedback; 100 strong corroborated current buying evidence across sources and periods. Historical clues do not establish current buying. Explain intermediate ratings: these are ordinal evidence-strength anchors, not precise/statistically meaningful demand differences or a validated sales forecast.

Other-GCC evidence can justify a UAE opportunity hypothesis without being UAE buying proof. Label each rating by supported country/model scope, for example 75 - KSA-supported, direct-comparable evidence. Strong evidence from one country is not GCC-wide validation. Never average unequally evidenced countries into a GCC-wide rating or transfer a country's rating to another; multiple supported countries mean multi-country evidence only. Global reviews with unknown buyer geography may inform quality/complaints/features, but not GCC purchasing; storefront geography alone does not establish buyer location.

In existing records, tag researched/transfer countries and UAE validation SUPPORTED / REGIONAL TRANSFER HYPOTHESIS / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE / CONTRADICTED / NOT ASSESSED. SUPPORTED needs country-attributed buying evidence for the labelled product/comparable scope, not launch approval. A transfer hypothesis needs another GCC market's evidence, a documented fit rationale and a named validation check. INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE means attempted research yielded inadequate buying evidence; NOT ASSESSED means meaningful country-specific assessment was not completed. CONTRADICTED requires adverse findings, not inaccessible sources. Mark unresearched countries NOT ASSESSED collectively; no additional six-country table/study is required.

Give HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW demand confidence based on buying-source quality, independence, recency and country/model match. Missing GCC UGC cannot lower demand confidence or rating. Keep creative evidence separate: GCC creator content helps localization; international, editorial or brand demos may support creative feasibility with provenance limits. Views alone are not sales. Relevant first-person buying discussions may support purchasing evidence, counted once, not as an extra social component.

There is no 80/20 blend, weighted demand-coverage percentage, mandatory creator quota or search-interest score. Preserve old scores as historical, not comparable market growth. Missing buying evidence remains UNKNOWN. Distinguish demand strength from quality/safety concerns and sample/launch readiness.

Only comparable dated buying indicators can support OBSERVED MOMENTUM, labelled by source, country, model and period. Otherwise use MOMENTUM NOT ESTABLISHED. One snapshot cannot prove growth or a market-wide trend; do not wait or repeatedly scrape to manufacture a time series.

Conclude STRONG DEMAND EVIDENCE / PROMISING DEMAND EVIDENCE / WEAK DEMAND EVIDENCE / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE for the explicit GCC country/model scope, followed by UAE validation status and next check. A demand verdict does not approve samples or launch.

### C. Checkpoint

List Product ID, completed gap, remaining questions and next action with supporting evidence references. If unfinished, include actual findings/URLs required to resume. Do not add a full dossier, supplier review or economics report.

Only when requested, add a separate creative appendix with up to three English hooks, three Arabic-friendly hooks and three demonstrations tied to observed patterns. Label creative suggestions as proposals; views do not establish that a hook converts.
