# UGC, search and demand evidence supplement

## Objective and inputs

Investigate demand for one candidate's solution and customer problem, while keeping exact-product evidence distinct. Use Product ID, original/current direct product URLs, exact variant, target GCC countries, prior evidence and optional Trends screenshots. Reuse available research and focus on a stated evidence gap; if none is specified, investigate the largest unresolved demand question.

This standalone tool supplies evidence to the workflow. It does not change Individual Prompt 2, override screening gates or approve a sample.

In Individual Prompt 2 v1.3, this is the P2-3 demand/UGC module. Reuse P2-2 marketplace findings and the current source register; research only missing or conflicting evidence, not a second complete market study. Return this module's evidence findings, demand assessment and checkpoint under the controller's stage budget. Creative proposals required by the main prompt may be appended compactly in that stage. Pause after P2-3; no final commercial approval or exports here. Standalone use below remains supported.

## Four evidence layers

| Layer | What to investigate | What it can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Exact product | Exact brand, model and variant | Traction for that identified product |
| Direct competitor | Comparable solution with a similar function | Demand for a competing solution, with differences disclosed |
| Category | Relevant category and alternative names | Interest in the broader solution class |
| Customer problem | Problem, routine, use case and desired benefit | Interest in the problem; purchase intent needs separate evidence |

Explain comparability. A sterilizer/dryer is not automatically equivalent to a bottle-washing machine; features, function and buying intent must match the claim. Category and problem evidence may support exploration but cannot satisfy an exact-product verification requirement.

## Focused research

1. Select approximately 8-12 English/Arabic queries across the four layers. Prioritize the target country, then useful GCC comparisons. Do not imply all six countries were researched if they were not.
2. Use the Prompt 2 v1.2 access protocol: start with up to four relevant queries and two accessible source types per target country from the query set. A second pass needs a named conflict or decision-changing gap. Record query, language, country, date, period and evidence type. Search visibility/autocomplete are directional; Trends is relative interest, not volume or sales. Trends/Keyword Planner are optional; stop retrying blocked sources, use accessible alternatives and record gaps without zero-filling.
3. Read supplied screenshots only for visible information. Record readable query, country, period, search type/category and capture date if shown. Mark truncated or hidden fields NOT CAPTURED. Do not invent chart values or describe a screenshot as independently retrieved data. For comparisons, disclose incompatible settings/time ranges.
4. Search useful creator platforms for exact product, competitors, category and problem demonstrations. Prefer up to six strong new posts overall; stop earlier when sufficient, and do not fill a quota with weak content. Reuse relevant existing references. Add more only for a named unresolved decision-critical question in a later run.
5. Open direct video destinations. Record creator/account, platform, publication date, visible views/likes/comments/shares, sponsorship status, hook, demonstration and evidence layer. Mark unavailable metrics NOT CAPTURED and inaccessible links NOT VERIFIED. Keep negative/safety content under risks. Mark posts older than 12 months HISTORICAL.
6. Keep GCC and international evidence separate. Creator location does not establish audience location. Record the basis of each geographical claim; global content can support creative/category potential without establishing GCC traction.
7. Reuse marketplace evidence and research only relevant gaps: target-country listing, price/currency, visible sales indicator, reviews, complaints, seller and date. Availability, review count and engagement are not confirmed sales. Do not count duplicate listings or reposted content as independent demand signals.

Calculate engagement only if views are positive and likes, comments and shares are all captured: `(likes + comments + shares) / views * 100`. Different platform metrics are not automatically comparable.

## Assessment and output

Maximum 900 words including tables, excluding URL strings. Return only:

### A. Evidence findings

| Evidence ID / direct source or screenshot | Layer / product match | Country and geographical basis | Date / period | Captured finding / metrics | Verification and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use up to ten strongest evidence rows total. Preserve any additional completed source records needed for the pending question in the checkpoint. Do not manufacture links for screenshots or inaccessible videos.

### B. Demand interpretation

State the best-supported exact-product, comparable/category and problem signals, country differences, purchasing evidence, main objections and unresolved gaps. Apply Demand Index v1.2: marketplace M 60%, search S 20%, GCC social L 20%. With all components assessed, report FULL: `M*0.60 + S*0.20 + L*0.20`, demand coverage 100%. With marketplace assessed and another component unavailable, divide assessed weighted points by assessed decimal weights; report PROVISIONAL with 60%/80% coverage. Without marketplace assessment, report INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE without a composite. Include assessed weak/negative signals rather than dropping them; never zero-fill unavailable data.

Use supported 0-100 component scores with evidence reasons and the Prompt 2 anchors: 25 isolated/stale signals, 50 some recent relevant evidence, 75 corroborated current signals, 100 strong current direct corroboration. Listing presence alone is capped at 25; no observations/access is UNKNOWN, not 0. Reviews are proxies, not sales counts. A partial score is not equivalent to the same full score. Keep global creative support and country indices separate. Preserve historical scores/versions and distinguish new evidence from changed weights.

Conclude STRONG DEMAND EVIDENCE / PROMISING DEMAND - GCC VALIDATION NEEDED / WEAK DEMAND EVIDENCE / INSUFFICIENT DATA - RESEARCH MORE. Explain what layer and geography the verdict covers. This is a demand verdict, not the final commercial decision.

### C. Checkpoint

List Product ID, completed gap, remaining questions and next action with supporting evidence references. If unfinished, include actual findings/URLs required to resume. Do not add a full dossier, supplier review or economics report.

Only when requested, add a separate creative appendix with up to three English hooks, three Arabic-friendly hooks and three demonstrations tied to observed patterns. Label creative suggestions as proposals; views do not establish that a hook converts.
