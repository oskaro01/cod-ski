# Evidence gap closure after Individual Prompt 2

## Purpose and inputs

Resolve decision-changing gaps for one product without restarting its research. Use the existing dossier, exports, source records, exact variant, target country/channel, latest decision, scores, confidence, economics and unresolved questions. Accept a user-approved 2A entry record where applicable; do not invent an earlier 1B record.

Use only records actually available in the conversation or supplied files. If a critical record is missing, identify it and continue only with work the available evidence supports. Preserve Product ID, Candidate ID when available, immutable Original Product URL, current Direct Product URL and verification history.

## Research procedure

1. Compare the latest dossier with subsequent evidence. Select at most three unresolved questions likely to change SAMPLE, RESEARCH MORE, HOLD or DROP. State any remaining questions in the checkpoint.
2. Reuse current, relevant verified sources. Recheck information when it is volatile, stale, conflicting or central to the decision. Prioritize identity, safety, sourcing, fulfilment and economics over cosmetic completeness.
3. Make one focused research pass per question; make a second only for a material conflict. Prefer direct product pages, manufacturer documents, primary regulatory sources and identifiable creator posts. Search results are leads until the underlying destination is verified.
4. Record each material finding with an evidence ID, direct URL or supplied-document reference, country, date checked, identity relevance and what it proves. Label VERIFIED, SUPPLIER-REPORTED, ESTIMATE, INFERENCE, UNKNOWN, NOT CAPTURED or PENDING. Supplier claims remain claims until independently supported.
5. If public research cannot resolve a question, specify the exact supplier quote, document, user-provided evidence or physical test needed, its owner and timing. Research does not establish a physical test result or approve a supplier transaction.
6. Update only conclusions and financial inputs affected by new evidence. Preserve original values and show dated revisions with reasons. Reuse the existing per-order economics and conservative/base/optimistic scenarios; show changed inputs and affected results rather than repeating the whole dossier.

## Scoring and decision controls

Use the current Individual Prompt 2 methodology and gates unchanged. Keep the 40/30/30 Demand Index, existing commercial weights, evidence coverage and confidence rules. If a demand component remains unavailable, retain INCOMPLETE; do not introduce 60/20/20, normalize weights or assume product-specific starting scores.

If no supported prior score exists, state NOT AVAILABLE. New evidence can support a dated reassessment under the same methodology. If the full assessment cannot be reproduced, label the result a proposed change and retain RESEARCH MORE where a decision-critical gap remains.

Exact-product, comparable-product, category and customer-problem findings must remain separately labelled. Global content is context for international traction or creative potential; it does not establish GCC buying demand.

## Output contract

Maximum 800 words including tables, excluding URL strings. Return only:

### A. Changes and remaining gaps

| Priority / gap | New finding and evidence reference | Status | Decision impact | Next action, owner and timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Include at most three researched questions. Preserve material unresolved risks in the conclusion or checkpoint even when not researched in this run.

### B. Assessment update

Show original versus revised decision, affected scores, confidence and coverage, with the assessment date. State UNCHANGED for unaffected fields. Separate demand-component availability from the existing item-by-item dossier evidence coverage. Show financial changes only when new inputs justify them; identify estimates and their source/assumptions.

### C. Next-stage readiness

Apply the actual Stage 3 entry requirements: complete dossier/export; no failed critical safety/compliance gate; plausible base-case contribution; defined country, channel, fulfilment model and test budget; exact variant; written sample test criteria. Record PASS/FAIL/PENDING with an evidence reference for each check. Do not mark an unresolved critical issue PASS merely because no failure was found.

State the next action. Only SAMPLE with satisfied entry requirements proceeds to Stage 3. Stage 3 conducts supplier outreach, payment verification, sample testing and later pilot/shipment approvals.

### D. Checkpoint

State Product ID, completed questions, remaining questions and source/evidence records to carry forward. Include actual newly captured findings and URLs if they are not already present above; never claim information was saved outside the returned response. If complete, state COMPLETE. Do not add an extra report after the checkpoint.
