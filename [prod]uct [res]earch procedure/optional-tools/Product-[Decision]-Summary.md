# Product decision summary

## Purpose and inputs

Explain one product's latest research decision in plain language. Use the available Individual Prompt 2 dossier and any later evidence supplement, the Product ID/exact variant, target country and latest dated decision. If identification is ambiguous, request the product URL or identifying record.

This is a summary task. Use supplied evidence; record missing research as a next action. Do not initiate a new research campaign or assign new scores.

In Individual Prompt 2 v1.3, run this module within P2-7 only after the controller has applied the full rubric, core coverage and gates and recorded its current decision. Summarize that decision in the existing 250-word limit. Keep the supporting core ledger, commercial ratings and gate evidence in the P2-7 record; the short summary does not replace them. Pause before P2-8 and do not produce exports here.

## Instructions

1. Distinguish the latest supported decision from historical decisions. Where later evidence conflicts without a completed reassessment, identify the conflict and keep the recommendation pending.
2. Explain what is promising using specific findings and source references. Separate GCC evidence from international evidence and exact-product evidence from comparable/category/problem evidence.
3. Identify the main reason to proceed, investigate, pause or stop. Missing evidence is an unresolved question; an observed failure is a negative finding.
4. Prioritize the exact information still needed. For each action, state who can obtain it, when it is needed and what decision it changes. Separate public research, supplier responses, pre-payment checks and physical sample tests. Do not require sample results before obtaining the sample.
5. Reuse the recorded economics and confidence. Do not turn product price spread into profit, assume unavailable supplier costs or treat a high demand score as sufficient for sampling.

## Decision language

| Recorded commercial decision | Plain-language explanation |
|---|---|
| SAMPLE | YES - proceed to Stage 3 entry checks and supplier/sample validation |
| RESEARCH MORE | MAYBE - resolve the named evidence gaps |
| HOLD | MAYBE - pause for the named timing, budget or execution dependency |
| DROP | NO - stop this opportunity for the documented reason |

If the input only contains a 1B YES/MAYBE/NO, label it a screening decision and say Individual Prompt 2 is still required. Never translate a screening YES directly into SAMPLE. If no supported decision exists, state DECISION NOT ESTABLISHED.

## Required output

Maximum 250 words per product, excluding URL strings. Use these sections only:

- PRODUCT: Product ID, exact variant, target country and latest research date.
- DECISION: Recorded decision and its plain-language meaning; one-sentence reason.
- PROMISING: Up to three specific, supported findings.
- MAIN RISKS: Up to three unresolved or confirmed risks, clearly distinguished. For NO, state the decisive rejection reason and whether it concerns sampling, inventory or both.
- COLLECT / NEXT ACTION: Up to five prioritized actions with owner and timing. If none remain, state the authorized next validation step. Refer to the existing detailed checklist for additional actions.

Use evidence IDs or direct source links for decision-changing facts. A summary does not authorize sample payment or inventory. If the evidence is insufficient, say what is unknown rather than creating a confident verdict.
