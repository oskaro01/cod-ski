## Research procedure

1. Compare the latest dossier with subsequent evidence. Select at most three unresolved questions likely to change SAMPLE, RESEARCH MORE, HOLD or DROP. State any remaining questions in the checkpoint.
2. Reuse current, relevant verified sources. Recheck information when it is volatile, stale, conflicting or central to the decision. Prioritize identity, safety, sourcing, fulfilment and economics over cosmetic completeness.
3. Make one focused research pass per question; make a second only for a material conflict. Prefer direct product pages, manufacturer documents, primary regulatory sources and identifiable creator posts. Search results are leads until the underlying destination is verified.
4. Record each material finding with an evidence ID, direct URL or supplied-document reference, country, date checked, identity relevance and what it proves. Label VERIFIED, SUPPLIER-REPORTED, ESTIMATE, INFERENCE, UNKNOWN, NOT CAPTURED or PENDING. Supplier claims remain claims until independently supported.
5. If public research cannot resolve a question, specify the exact supplier quote, document, user-provided evidence or physical test needed, its owner and timing. Research does not establish a physical test result or approve a supplier transaction.
6. Update only conclusions and financial inputs affected by new evidence. Preserve original values and show dated revisions with reasons. Reuse the existing per-order economics and conservative/base/optimistic scenarios; show changed inputs and affected results rather than repeating the whole dossier.

## Scoring and decision controls

Use the main Prompt 2's qualitative public-market traction method: ENCOURAGING / MIXED / WEAK / UNASSESSABLE, with facts/source IDs, current/historical and exact/comparable scope, separate HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW evidence confidence, UAE validation and a next action. No numerical demand score or points. One credible activity source can justify research; independent corroboration strengthens confidence, not a fixed two-source quota. Label unknown buyer geography MARKETPLACE-ASSOCIATED, not confirmed residency; global/imported pools stay GLOBAL/POOLED. Missing access is not WEAK traction. Other-GCC/global activity can support a labelled UAE transfer hypothesis, not proven UAE demand; missing GCC UGC is not a demand penalty.

Apply the four commercial weights (creative 15, economics 30, competition 10, supplier/execution 25), totalling 80 without normalization or redistributed demand points. Keep traction separate, the 15-item core checklist and 80% core minimum with every sample-entry requirement. D1/D2 assess GCC buying evidence and explicit UAE relevance/validation. A bounded SAMPLE may use a defensible regional transfer case; UAE commercial pilot dependencies remain unresolved until actually validated. U1 requires a relevant inspected demo, not a GCC creator quota. No weighted demand coverage applies. Search-interest tools are excluded; source-finding searches are not demand evidence.

If the current full Prompt 2 rubric is unavailable, request it; do not reconstruct its gates or grant SAMPLE from a partial reassessment. Preserve older scores/denominators as historical. Reassess affected traction and commercial values from actual evidence; distinguish methodology changes from new findings. Never convert historical /100 scores to /80 or infer traction from an old index. When the assessment cannot be reproduced, label it a proposed change and retain RESEARCH MORE where a critical gap remains.

Exact-product, comparable-product, category and customer-problem findings must remain separately labelled. Global content is context for international traction or creative potential; it does not establish GCC buying demand.
