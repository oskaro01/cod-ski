## Demand Calculation

`Demand Index = marketplace M * 0.80 + local social/community L * 0.20`

M evaluates target-country purchasing/customer evidence. Prefer a platform-reported sold/bought indicator with its exact stated scope and period, then recent dated product-specific buyer feedback. Relevant independent competitor offers corroborate demand only when they carry separate purchasing/feedback evidence. High star averages indicate reported satisfaction; listing/seller counts show availability. Neither alone proves current unit sales or profitability.

L evaluates inspectable local creator/customer use and meaningful response with geographical support. Reposts, brand promotion and global engagement cannot establish independent local traction. A buyer discussion already scored under M is not counted again as an extra L signal. Record scope, dates, model/variant and source independence.

Google Trends, Keyword Planner, keyword volumes, autocomplete, CPC and search visibility are excluded from scoring and research requirements. Ordinary web searches remain available to find product, customer, supplier and creator sources. Search results are leads until inspected.

Both components assessed: FULL, 100% coverage. M assessed/L missing: PROVISIONAL - MARKETPLACE ONLY, score M, 80% coverage. M missing: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, no composite. An assessed poor L score stays in the calculation. Missing evidence is never zero. Missing L caps screening demand confidence at MEDIUM and Prompt 2 confidence at B; it alone does not force rejection.

The 80/20 weights are a workflow heuristic, not a statistically calibrated forecast. Old 60/20/20 scores stay historical. Recalculate from source evidence and identify the methodology change; a higher new score is not proof of market growth.

## Screening and Readiness

Screening execution 1.2 makes the five-item evidence checklist the primary advancement method: identity, local purchasing activity, independent corroboration, customer-quality concerns and sourcing/offer route. Record supported findings, gaps and documented failures with references; do not create an additional score or percentage. The 80/20 index remains a comparison aid and cannot override these checks. This delivery clarification applies to the 15-product screening file, not a reduction of Prompt 2's deep research or detailed exports.

The screening output now distinguishes opportunity strength from sourcing/economic readiness. A credible brand/distributor inquiry route can support further research even when wholesale terms are private. Named branded products use branded resale routes; private-label products use manufacturer/specification matching. Similar-looking items do not inherit a branded product's demand, reviews or identity.

A research YES still needs established identity, a clear problem, independent local demand evidence, a supported demonstration case, a credible sourcing route and no unresolved critical blocker. Missing quote-only economics are explicit dependencies rather than an automatic rejection. A sourced economic failure or serious unresolved product risk cannot be hidden by demand. Individual Prompt 2 retains stricter SAMPLE economics and evidence gates; no purchase approval follows from screening.

Each record identifies inspected, limited, blocked, externally dependent or unattempted checks. Readable reviews must be inspected; store-wide feedback and global pools are labelled. Empty video players/metadata are not inspected demonstrations. Old cached values retain their original date/scope. A conditional recovery stage targets up to two candidates per response, with named decision-changing gaps; private quotes/tests become owned follow-ups rather than repeated public searches.
