## GCC Market Scope and Research Rules

This research is specifically focused on the six GCC markets:

1. United Arab Emirates (UAE)
2. Saudi Arabia (KSA)
3. Kuwait
4. Qatar
5. Bahrain
6. Oman

All product research, market validation, demand assessment, competitor analysis, pricing research, customer feedback analysis, and commercial evaluation must be conducted with these GCC markets as the target market.

- Prioritize GCC-specific marketplaces, retailers, suppliers, customers, search trends, and social-commerce evidence.

- Record the country, platform, currency, source URL, and research date for relevant findings.

- Do not treat non-GCC marketplace listings, ratings, reviews, sales figures, or popularity as proof of GCC demand or GCC customer satisfaction.

- International sources may be used for supporting technical specifications, manufacturer information, product documentation, and global supplier discovery, but clearly distinguish them from GCC market evidence.

- Suppliers may be located outside the GCC, provided their ability to supply the target GCC market is properly evaluated.

- Assess GCC-specific shipping, landed costs, delivery feasibility, returns, warranty, product compliance, and applicable country requirements.

- Never assume that evidence from one GCC country automatically applies to all six countries.

- If GCC-specific evidence is unavailable, explicitly record the gap rather than substituting unrelated international evidence.

## 3. Objective

Commercially pre-screen the Stage 0.2 pool and identify approximately
**20--30 candidates** suitable to enter the existing Prompt 1A.

The target is not a quota. If only 14 candidates have credible
commercial evidence, pass 14. If more than 30 qualify, rank them by
evidence-backed score and keep the remainder as a reserve list. Never
lower the evidence standard to fill a target. Track A and Track B may
both be selected for Prompt 1A when commercial evidence supports them,
but retain the Stage 0.2 track label and do not combine their Stage 0.2
scores or describe Track B as quality-qualified.

Every candidate must receive one decision:

-   **SELECT FOR PROMPT 1A:** credible commercial opportunity for deeper
    discovery and verification.
-   **RESERVE / RESEARCH:** promising, but a specific commercial
    uncertainty remains.
-   **REJECT:** evidence indicates weak demand, impossible or
    unattractive economics, no credible differentiation, infeasible
    sourcing/fulfilment or a critical blocker.
-   **UNPROCESSED:** a source/tool dependency prevented a fair
    assessment; record the exact recovery action.

### Stage 0.2 track-preservation rules

- Carry `TRACK A — REVIEW EVIDENCE AVAILABLE` or `TRACK B — NO REVIEW

- Carry the Stage 0.2 decision and score type separately. Track A uses

- Stage 0.3 may select a Track B candidate for Prompt 1A if its commercial

## 4. Commercial pre-screen dimensions

Evaluate the following seven dimensions.

### A. Marketplace and cross-channel demand --- 20 points

Check: - Direct UAE and Saudi listings where available. - Other GCC
marketplace and retailer listings. - Visible price, currency, stock and
delivery signals. - Ratings and review counts captured in Stage 0.2,
where available. - Category ranking or disclosed sales indicators. -
Independent seller/listing presence. - Search interest and purchase-
intent queries. - Social discussion and customer demand signals. -
Whether the evidence is product-specific, similar-product, category-
level or general trend.

For Track B products, the absence of customer reviews is an evidence
gap, not proof of zero demand. Assess demand using the other available
signals: current listings and availability, independent seller presence,
rankings or disclosed sales indicators, purchase-intent search evidence,
social discussion and other observable customer-demand signals. Do not
invent or infer sales from listing presence alone. If the demand evidence
itself is missing, weak or contradictory, score that dimension
accordingly or mark it insufficient; do not award points merely because
reviews are absent.

Do not turn rankings, reviews or marketplace badges into invented sales
volumes. Multiple listings copied from one seller are not independent
demand confirmation.

### B. GCC market suitability --- 15 points

Check all six countries separately:

1.  UAE
2.  Saudi Arabia
3.  Kuwait
4.  Qatar
5.  Bahrain
6.  Oman

For each country, record: - Direct product or category listing
evidence. - Local price and currency. - Local alternatives and
availability. - Relevant lifestyle, climate, seasonality or usage fit. -
English/Arabic search wording where accessible. - Delivery, COD, returns
and local fulfilment signals. - Country-specific restrictions or
documentation concerns.

UAE and KSA are the core validation markets. Evidence in one country
does not prove demand in another. Do not claim GCC-wide validation from
one regional marketplace listing.

### C. Preliminary economics --- 20 points

Capture raw, visible or clearly labelled estimated inputs:

-   GCC competitor selling prices and currency.
-   Supplier product prices and currency.
-   MOQ and price breaks.
-   Product weight/dimensions where available.
-   Inbound freight signal or missing quote.
-   Packaging, inspection and fulfilment assumptions.
-   VAT/customs considerations requiring later verification.
-   Likely return, damage or failed-delivery risks.
-   Whether the apparent selling price leaves a plausible spread.

Calculate only:

``` text
Preliminary product spread = competitor selling price - visible supplier product cost
```

If currencies differ, show original amounts and the exchange-rate
source/date used for any conversion.

This spread is not profit. Do not claim a product is profitable based on
this calculation. Do not present guessed freight, duties, VAT, platform
fees or advertising costs as verified quotes.

### D. Competition and differentiation --- 10 points

Check: - Number of meaningful GCC competitors. - Exact versus comparable
products. - Current selling-price range. - Brand strength and market
saturation. - Offer/bundle differences. - Common complaints and market
gaps. - Potential differentiation through quality, bundle, warranty,
instructions, customer service or positioning.

Do not call a market unsaturated merely because few search results were
found.

### E. Supplier and sourcing feasibility --- 15 points

Look for direct product-level supplier leads from: - Alibaba. - Global
Sources. - Made-in-China. - DHgate. - Manufacturer websites. - Wholesale
catalogues.

Record: - Direct URL. - Supplier name. - Exact match, comparable match,
discovery lead or not found. - Visible price, original currency, MOQ and
price breaks. - Sample availability. - Product specifications and
included components. - Production and shipping signals. - Relevant
certificates/documents claimed by the supplier. - Supplier verification
limitations.

A supplier search page or profile is a discovery lead, not a verified
direct product listing. Do not approve a supplier in this stage.

### F. UGC and creative potential --- 10 points

Check: - Direct creator/customer demonstrations. - TikTok, Instagram
Reels, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat Spotlight, Pinterest and public
communities. - GCC creator relevance where verifiable. - Publication
date and visible views/interactions. - Exact product, similar product,
category or trend classification. - Demonstration clarity and customer
problem shown. - Comments that reveal objections or purchase intent.

Do not invent metrics or creator audience countries. Do not classify a
product as high demand because it is easy to demonstrate. Keep creative
potential separate from observed demand.

### G. Fulfilment, safety and operational feasibility --- 10 points

Check: - Fragility, dimensions, weight and storage. - Battery,
electrical, chemical, food-contact, skin-contact or child-safety issues
as relevant. - Dangerous-goods or shipping complexity. - Customs and
conformity documentation likely to be needed. - Returns, warranty and
replacement practicality. - Customer misuse and support burden. -
Whether a reasonable GCC delivery path appears possible.

Mark documentation and regulatory issues `PENDING VERIFICATION`. A
critical safety or legal blocker cannot be offset by a high score.

## 5. Commercial pre-screen score

Score each dimension from 0 to 100 and calculate:

``` text
Commercial Pre-Screen Score =
(Marketplace and cross-channel demand × 0.20)
+ (GCC market suitability × 0.15)
+ (Preliminary economics × 0.20)
+ (Competition and differentiation × 0.10)
+ (Supplier and sourcing feasibility × 0.15)
+ (UGC and creative potential × 0.10)
+ (Fulfilment, safety and operational feasibility × 0.10)
```

Scoring anchors: - 0: no credible evidence or contradicted. - 25: weak,
isolated, indirect or outdated. - 50: some relevant evidence, but
limited or mixed. - 75: multiple consistent and relevant signals. - 100:
strong, current, direct evidence from multiple independent sources.

Suggested interpretation: - 80--100: priority candidate. - 70--79:
strong candidate. - 60--69: reserve/research. - 50--59: weak; normally
reject. - Below 50: reject unless the evidence is too incomplete to
judge, in which case use UNPROCESSED or RESERVE with a specific recovery
action.

A high score never overrides a critical safety, legal, sourcing or
fulfilment blocker.

  C05    Weak search or purchase-intent evidence

  C12    Fragile, high-return or high-support product
  C13    Seasonal or timing concern
  C14    Insufficient evidence; specific research needed

A product can have multiple reason codes. Every rejection or reserve
decision must explain the evidence and the exact check that could change
the outcome.

VERIFIED** - **NOT CAPTURED** - **PENDING** - **N/A** only when
genuinely not applicable, with a reason.

For every material finding, record: - Direct URL. - Source ID. - Source
type and discovery channel. - Country and platform/publisher. -
Publication/update date when available. - Date checked. - Exact product,
similar product, category or trend relevance. - What the source actually
proves.

Reopen important Stage 0.1/0.2 sources. Refresh time-sensitive prices
and listings. Do not repeat research without a reason, but do not assume
an old listing is current.
