# Seller Onboarding Verification

Use this skill for vendor acquisition and approval workflows.

## Khaleeja Seller Positioning

- Verified women-owned GCC brands.
- Low marketplace commission can be used as a seller acquisition advantage.
- Sellers need trust, exposure, simple onboarding, payment clarity, and shipping support.

## Verification Checklist

- Founder name and ID where legally appropriate.
- Store/business name.
- Country and city.
- Trade license or proof of business.
- Social/shop links.
- Product category.
- Product photos.
- Fulfilment capability.
- Confirmation that business is women-owned.
- Annual verification renewal.

## Onboarding Flow

1. Seller applies.
2. Admin reviews documents.
3. Seller gets approved or asked for more info.
4. Seller creates store profile.
5. Seller uploads products.
6. Admin reviews first products.
7. Store goes live.

## Output Style

Create checklists, application forms, email/WhatsApp scripts, and simple SOPs.
