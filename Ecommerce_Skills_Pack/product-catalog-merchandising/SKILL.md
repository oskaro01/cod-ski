# Product Catalog Merchandising

Use this skill to make products easier to browse and buy.

## Khaleeja Priority Categories

- Oud, perfume, bakhoor
- Candles and home scent
- Abayas and modest fashion
- Jewelry
- Ramadan and Eid gifting
- Majlis and home ritual
- Wellness and self-care
- Premium handmade gifts

## Collections To Create

- New Arrivals
- Bestsellers
- Under AED 150
- Eid Gifts
- Ramadan Gifts
- Made in UAE
- Saudi Women-Owned Brands
- Perfume and Bakhoor Sets
- Self-Care Gifts

## Merchandising Rules

- Use filters shoppers understand: category, price, country, seller, delivery time, gift type.
- Use bundles to increase average order value.
- Avoid too many empty categories.
- Do not run heavy paid ads until catalog depth is strong enough.
