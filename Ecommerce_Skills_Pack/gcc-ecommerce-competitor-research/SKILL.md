# GCC Ecommerce Competitor Research

Use this skill for competitor analysis across Gulf ecommerce brands and marketplaces.

## Research Scope

Common competitor fields:

- Brand name and URL
- Country/market coverage
- Positioning
- Product categories
- Target customer
- Seller/vendor proposition
- Shipping policy
- Courier/delivery company
- Payment gateway and payment methods
- Returns/refunds
- Pricing and free shipping thresholds
- SEO/content strategy
- Trust signals
- UX strengths and weaknesses
- Threat level
- Opportunity for Khaleeja

## Evidence Rules

Always separate:

- Confirmed public evidence
- Inferred evidence
- Unknown/not public

For unstable information such as shipping prices, payment gateways, delivery companies, product pricing, and policies, verify with current public sources or checkout observation before answering.

Do not claim a payment gateway or courier is confirmed unless it appears in:

- Public policy page
- Checkout page
- Network/domain evidence
- Tracking URL
- Order confirmation
- Public footer/payment badges with provider signal

## Checkout Inspection Boundary

Permitted:

- Open product/cart/checkout pages manually or with browser automation.
- Stop before payment submission.
- Inspect visible page text, URLs, public scripts, and request domains.
- Record payment/courier signals.

Not permitted:

- Bypass access controls
- Submit payment without explicit user instruction
- Attack, probe vulnerabilities, or evade protections
- Access private customer/seller data
