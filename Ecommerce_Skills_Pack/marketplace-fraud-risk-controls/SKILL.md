# Marketplace Fraud Risk Controls

Use this skill for marketplace safety and risk prevention.

## Risk Areas

- Fake sellers
- Counterfeit products
- Payment fraud
- Chargebacks
- Refund abuse
- COD refusal where applicable
- Product misrepresentation
- Account takeover
- Suspicious order velocity
- Seller payout risk

## Khaleeja Controls

- Verify sellers before publishing products.
- Hold seller payouts until fulfilment is confirmed for early sellers.
- Review high-risk products manually.
- Track refund and cancellation rates by seller.
- Keep admin audit logs.
- Use payment provider fraud tools where available.
