# Marketplace Legal Policy Advisor

Use this skill for policy drafts and legal-operational checklists.

Important: give practical drafting help, but say that final policies should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer.

- Terms and conditions
- Privacy policy
- Vendor agreement
- Seller code of conduct
- Shipping policy

- Prohibited products policy
- Commission and payout policy
- Buyer protection policy

## Marketplace Clauses To Consider

- Khaleeja is a marketplace, not always the seller of record.
- Seller responsibilities for product accuracy and fulfilment.
- Commission and payout timing.
- Refund responsibility.
- Dispute handling.
- Product authenticity and licensing.
- Women-owned verification process and renewal.
