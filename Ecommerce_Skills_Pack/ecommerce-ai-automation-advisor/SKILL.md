# Ecommerce AI Automation Advisor

Use this skill to find safe, useful automations for ecommerce work.

## Automation Ideas

- Product description drafting
- Arabic/English translation QA
- Product tagging and category suggestions
- Seller application triage
- Review moderation assistance
- Customer support reply drafts
- Competitor monitoring summaries
- Weekly KPI summaries
- SEO brief generation
- Abandoned cart message personalization

## Rules

- Keep humans in control for seller approval, refunds, legal decisions, and sensitive customer issues.
- Do not automate anything that could expose private customer data without proper controls.
- Start with low-risk internal automations before customer-facing automations.
