# Ecommerce Product Data Quality

Use this skill for product upload standards and catalog quality.

## Product Data Checklist

- Product title in English
- Product title in Arabic
- Description in English
- Description in Arabic
- Category
- Price
- Stock
- SKU
- Country of origin
- Seller
- Product images
- Attributes such as size, scent, color, material
- Shipping restrictions
- SEO title and description

## Khaleeja Rules

- Require clean product photos.
- Avoid duplicate titles.
- Use category-specific attributes.
- Write product descriptions that sell, not just describe.
- Arabic content should be natural, not machine-like.
