## What This Setup Means

The unified inbox collects social messages, comments, and customer questions from Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok into one Ecommerized inbox. Instead of checking each app separately, the brand can review all questions in one queue, tag them by topic, auto-reply to common questions, and escalate sensitive issues to a human.

## Inbox Tags

Use these tags to organize messages inside the unified inbox.

| Tag | Use For |
|---|---|
| `product-question` | Questions about what the product is, how it works, or what comes in the pack |
| `price-link` | Questions asking for price, product link, or how to buy |
| `care-instructions` | Questions about rinsing, drying, cleaning, or product care |
| `shipping-status` | Questions about order tracking, delivery time, or shipping |
| `return-refund` | Return, refund, cancellation, or exchange requests |
| `damaged-wrong-item` | Damaged product, wrong item, or missing item complaints |
| `human-needed` | Anything that requires manual review |
| `positive-comment` | Compliments, thank-you comments, or positive product reactions |
| `spam-hide` | Spam, unrelated promotions, or harmful comments |

## Inbox Priority Rules

| Priority | Message Type | Response Target |
|---|---|---|
| High | Damaged item, wrong item, refund request, angry complaint, payment issue | Human reply within 4 business hours |
| Medium | Shipping status, product questions, care instructions | Auto-reply instantly, human follow-up within 1 business day if needed |
| Low | Positive comments, general engagement, emoji-only comments | Auto-reply or manual community reply within 1-2 business days |
| Hide/Review | Spam, offensive language, unrelated promotions | Hide or mark for review |

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|

# Human Escalation Rules

Auto-replies should not handle refunds, order changes, payment issues, damaged items, legal threats, influencer partnerships, or emotional complaints without human review.

| Escalate When | Reason |
|---|---|
| Customer shares order number | Needs private order lookup |
| Customer asks for refund/return | Policy and order details must be checked |
| Customer reports damaged/wrong item | Needs photo and manual support decision |
| Customer asks about certifications or guarantees | Avoid unsupported claims |
| Customer is upset or threatening chargeback | Needs careful human handling |
| Customer asks for bulk/wholesale order | Sales opportunity needs custom response |

# Brand Voice Rules for Replies

- Be warm, short, and helpful.
- Use plain language.
- Move private order details into DM.
- Do not invent policies, discounts, shipping timelines, certifications, or guarantees.
- Do not shame customers for using paper towels.
- Keep the message practical: reusable, simple, daily, less wasteful.
