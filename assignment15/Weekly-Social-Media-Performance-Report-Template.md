- Use the same date range across all platforms.
- Compare every week against the previous week.
- Do not report revenue or orders unless Ecommerized tracking confirms attribution.
- If a metric is unavailable, write `Not available` instead of guessing.
- Add screenshots of dashboards or top posts when submitting the weekly report.

## Metric Definitions

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Reach | Number of unique users who saw the content |
| Impressions/views | Total number of times content was shown or viewed |
| Engagements | Likes, comments, shares, saves, and other interactions |
| Engagement rate | Engagements divided by reach or impressions |
| Link clicks | Clicks from social content to store/product page |
| Product-page visits | Sessions on the product page from social traffic |
| Orders attributed to social | Orders credited to social channels by Ecommerized tracking |
| Average first response time | Average time between customer message and first brand response |
