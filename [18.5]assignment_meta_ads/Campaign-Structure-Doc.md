# Meta Ad Campaign Structure Doc

## Campaign Overview

**Brand:** CleanNest Kitchen  
**Product:** Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack  
**Store type:** Eco-friendly kitchen cleaning products  
**Product link:** `/products/reusable-swedish-dishcloth-10-pack`  
**Primary campaign objective:** Sales / website purchases  
**Conversion event:** Purchase  
**Primary CTA:** Shop Now  
**Campaign goal:** Generate profitable purchases while building a retargeting audience for future campaigns.

## Brand Voice and Positioning

CleanNest Kitchen is practical, warm, clear, and low-pressure. The brand helps shoppers make simple low-waste kitchen swaps without guilt or exaggerated environmental promises.

**Core message:** Make everyday kitchen cleanup less wasteful and easier to keep up with.

**Product value points:**

- Reusable Swedish dishcloths for everyday kitchen wipe-downs
- 10-pack format makes weekly rotation easier
- Useful for counters, sink splashes, coffee rings, crumbs, and table resets
- Practical paper towel alternative for small daily messes
- Simple routine: wet, wring, wipe, rinse, dry

**Claim safety rules:**

- Do not claim "zero waste," "chemical-free," "guaranteed," or "best" unless verified.
- Do not invent reviews, discounts, environmental certifications, or performance claims.
- Keep the message practical: reusable, simple, daily, less wasteful.

# 2. Budget Allocation Plan

## Daily Budget Split

| Funnel stage | Ad set | Budget % | Daily budget | 14-day budget | Reason |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| TOF | Cold Interest Audience | 50% | `$25/day` | `$350` | Largest audience pool; feeds the funnel with new visitors and engagement |
| MOF | Engaged / Video / Social Audience | 30% | `$15/day` | `$210` | Warmer audience needs education and repeated touchpoints |
| BOF | Website Retargeting / Cart Intent | 20% | `$10/day` | `$140` | Smaller but highest-intent audience; avoid overspending too early |
| **Total** |  | **100%** | **`$50/day`** | **`$700`** |  |

## Budget Rules

- Run the campaign for at least 3 days before making major changes unless tracking is broken.
- Shift 10-15% more budget toward any ad set with the lowest cost per add-to-cart or purchase after enough data is collected.
- Do not scale a winning ad set by more than 20-30% per day during the test period.
- Pause individual creatives with weak click-through rate, high cost per landing page view, or no meaningful engagement after a fair test.
- Keep BOF spend controlled because the retargeting pool will be small at the start.

## KPI Targets for Evaluation

| Funnel stage | Main KPI | Secondary KPI |
|---|---|---|
| TOF | Cost per landing page view | CTR, thumb-stop rate, engagement rate |
| MOF | Cost per add-to-cart | Landing page views, content views, saves, clicks |
| BOF | Cost per purchase | ROAS, checkout starts, add-to-cart rate |

# 6. Creative Testing Matrix

| Funnel | Creative 1 angle | Creative 2 angle | Creative 3 angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOF | Paper towel alternative | Everyday messes | Simple reusable swap |
| MOF | Weekly rotation | How it works | Coffee corner use case |
| BOF | Product reminder | Cart/product return | Objection handling |

## Testing Notes

- Test three distinct creative angles per ad set instead of small wording changes.
- Keep the product visible in every creative.
- Use short copy for Feed placements and even shorter overlays for Stories/Reels.
- Avoid overloading image text; let the visual show the product and the ad copy explain the benefit.
- If one creative wins across multiple ad sets, adapt it by funnel stage instead of copying it unchanged.

# 7. Measurement and Optimization Plan

## First 72 Hours

- Confirm the Meta Pixel is tracking content views, add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase events.
- Check delivery across placements.
- Do not pause ads too early unless there is a tracking issue, policy rejection, or obvious creative error.
- Monitor CTR, cost per landing page view, and early add-to-cart signals.

## Days 4-7

- Pause creatives with the weakest engagement and no meaningful site activity.
- Keep at least one creative active per ad set.
- Move budget toward the ad set producing the best cost per add-to-cart or purchase.
- Check whether BOF frequency is too high. If it is, refresh creative or reduce BOF budget.

## Days 8-14

- Promote winning creative angles into new variations.
- If TOF produces cheap traffic but low add-to-cart activity, test stronger product education.
- If MOF drives add-to-cart but not purchase, test clearer product-page offer and BOF messaging.
- If BOF converts, gradually increase budget and add new retargeting creative to prevent fatigue.

## Decision Rules

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| High CTR, low add-to-cart | Improve landing page or test more product-specific creative |
| Low CTR, high CPM | Refresh hook, visual, or audience |
| High add-to-cart, low purchase | Improve BOF ads, checkout trust, shipping clarity, or offer |
| BOF high frequency, low purchase | Reduce BOF budget or refresh creative |
| One creative clearly wins | Create 2-3 new variants around the same angle |
