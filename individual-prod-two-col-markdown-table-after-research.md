# PRODUCT RESEARCH – SPREADSHEET OUTPUT FORMAT

After completing product research, present all findings in a clean, easy-to-copy, two-column Markdown table.

**Table structure:**

| Column | Information to enter |
|---|---|
| Product Name | Product's complete name |
| Category | Relevant product category and subcategory |
| Competitor/Product Link | Direct product listing URL |
| Supplier Link | Direct supplier/product URL |
| Product Cost (USD) | Supplier unit price, including MOQ and whether shipping is excluded |
| Competitor Selling Price (USD) | Competitor's current selling price converted to USD, with original currency in brackets |
| UGC Video/Post Link | Direct links to relevant UGC videos or posts |
| UGC Views / Engagement | Available views, likes, comments, engagement information and number of videos found |
| Trend / Demand | Marketplace ratings, review counts, sales indicators, search trends and relevant demand signals |
| Research Decision | Clear decision such as TEST CANDIDATE, CONDITIONAL TEST, HOLD, RESEARCH MORE or DROP |
| Notes | All additional research findings and important information |

## Formatting Rules

1. Always use the exact 11 column names listed above.
2. Present the information in a two-column table: **Column** and **Information to enter**.
3. Put each spreadsheet field in its own row.
4. Keep the main fields concise, clear and easy to copy individually.
5. Put ALL additional findings inside the **Notes** row. Do not create a separate additional findings table.
6. The Notes field should consolidate relevant information, including:
   - Product specifications and features.
   - Marketplace ratings, reviews and sales indicators.
   - Competitor names and prices.
   - Supplier names, ratings, MOQ, pricing and performance indicators.
   - UGC findings and missing engagement metrics.
   - Demand and trend observations.
   - Product-only gross spread or preliminary economics.
   - Screening score and confidence grade.
   - Quality, compliance, fulfilment and sourcing risks.
   - Missing information requiring verification.
   - Recommended next action.
7. Use bold labels inside Notes to separate information, for example: **PRODUCT:**, **NOON PERFORMANCE:**, **SUPPLIER:**, **COMPETITION:**, **UGC:**, **ECONOMICS:**, **DEMAND:**, **SCREENING SCORE:**, **RISKS:** and **NEXT ACTION:**.
8. Include direct URLs rather than vague references such as "Amazon listing" or "Alibaba supplier".
9. Clearly identify unknown or unverified information. Never invent missing values.
10. Do not add unnecessary explanations before or after the table.

**Final requirement:** Deliver exactly one complete, spreadsheet-style table containing all 11 fields. All supplementary findings must be consolidated into the Notes field so I can transfer the information directly into my existing Google Sheet or Excel spreadsheet without rearranging anything.
