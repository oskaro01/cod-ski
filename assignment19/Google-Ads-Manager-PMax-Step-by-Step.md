# Google Ads Manager Account and Performance Max Campaign: Step-by-Step Guide

## Assignment Overview

**Assignment task:** Create a Google Ads Manager account, create a Google Ads account, and draft a Performance Max campaign.  
**Main URL:** https://business.google.com/uk/ad-tools/manage-accounts/  
**Brand for campaign draft:** Your Product  
**Product:** Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack  
**Product page:** `/products/reusable-swedish-dishcloth-10-pack`  
**Campaign type:** Performance Max  
**Goal:** Sales or website traffic  
**Important note:** Do not publish/spend money unless your instructor specifically asks you to launch ads.

## What This Assignment Means

This assignment has three parts:

1. Create a Google Ads Manager account. This is like a parent account for managing ad accounts.
2. Create a Google Ads account under that manager account. This is the actual account where campaigns are made.
3. Draft a Performance Max campaign. This campaign can show across Google inventory such as Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail, and Maps.

Google says a manager account is used to manage multiple Google Ads accounts from one place. Google also says Performance Max is a goal-based campaign type that uses Google AI across Google Ads inventory.

# Part 1: Create a Google Ads Manager Account

## Step 1: Open the Manager Account Page

Go to:

https://business.google.com/uk/ad-tools/manage-accounts/

If that link redirects, you can also use:

https://ads.google.com/home/tools/manager-accounts/

## Step 2: Sign In

Sign in using the Google account you want to use for this assignment.

Use an account you can access later, because you may need to return for screenshots.

## Step 3: Click "Create a Manager Account"

Look for a button such as:

- `Create a manager account`
- `Go to Manager Accounts`
- `Start now`

The exact label may vary by Google interface, but the goal is to start creating a manager account.

## Step 4: Enter Manager Account Details

Use these values:

| Field | Recommended Entry |
|---|---|
| Account name | `Your Product Manager Account` |
| Primary account use | Manage my own Google Ads accounts |
| Country | Your country or assignment target country |
| Time zone | Your actual time zone |
| Currency | Your assignment currency or USD |

Do not put a website URL inside the account name. Google says URLs are no longer accepted in account names.

## Step 5: Submit

Click:

- `Submit`
- `Save`
- `Explore your account`

# Part 2: Create a Google Ads Account Under the Manager Account

## Step 1: Open the Manager Account Dashboard

Go to:

https://ads.google.com/

Make sure you are inside the manager account, not a normal ad account.

## Step 2: Go to Accounts

In the left menu, look for:

- `Accounts`
- `Sub-account settings`
- `Performance`

Google's official flow says to click the plus button on the Accounts page and choose `Create new account`.

## Step 3: Click the Blue Plus Button

Click the blue `+` button.

Choose:

`Create new account`

Do not choose `Link existing account` unless you already have a separate Google Ads account.

## Step 4: Add Business Information

Use these values:

| Field | Recommended Entry |
|---|---|
| Business name | `Your Product` |
| Website URL | Your live store URL |
| Account name | `Your Product Google Ads Account` |
| Country | Same as manager account |
| Time zone | Same as manager account |
| Currency | Same as manager account |

Google may ask for business information like business name and URL to personalize the setup flow.

## Step 5: Link Optional Accounts

Google may ask if you want to link:

- Google Merchant Center
- YouTube channel
- Google Business Profile

For this assignment:

| Account | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Merchant Center | Link it if already created; otherwise skip and connect later |
| YouTube | Skip unless you have a brand channel |
| Google Business Profile | Skip unless you have a local business profile |

## Step 6: Billing Warning

Google may ask for payment/billing details.

If the assignment only asks for a draft, do not spend money. If billing is required before continuing, stop and take a screenshot showing the setup progress.

# Part 3: Draft a Performance Max Campaign

## Step 1: Start a New Campaign

Inside the Google Ads account, go to:

`Campaigns`

Click:

`+ New campaign`

## Step 2: Choose Campaign Objective

Recommended objective:

`Sales`

If Google will not allow Sales because conversion tracking is missing, choose:

`Website traffic`

Use this decision:

| Situation | Choose |
|---|---|
| Purchase conversion tracking is set up | Sales |
| No purchase tracking yet | Website traffic |
| Teacher only needs campaign draft | Website traffic is acceptable |

## Step 3: Choose Campaign Type

Select:

`Performance Max`

Google says Performance Max can be used for Sales, Leads, Website traffic, Local store visits and promotions, or without a goal's guidance.

## Step 4: Select Conversion Goal

If available, select:

`Purchase`

If purchase tracking is not available, use:

`Page view`

or:

`Website traffic`

Do not make up conversions if tracking is not installed.

## Step 5: Add Website/Product URL

Use the live full product URL. Example:

```text
https://yourstore.com/products/reusable-swedish-dishcloth-10-pack
```

Replace `https://yourstore.com` with your real store domain.

## Step 6: Campaign Name

Use:

```text
PMax | Your Product | Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack | Sales
```

## Step 7: Bidding

Recommended bidding:

`Maximize conversions`

If Google asks about target CPA or target ROAS:

- Leave target CPA blank for a beginner test.
- Leave target ROAS blank unless you already know your profitable ROAS.

Why: a new campaign usually needs data before tight targets are useful.

## Step 8: Budget

Use a small draft/test budget:

```text
$10 per day
```

If your assignment uses a different currency, use the equivalent small daily amount.

Important: If you are not launching the ad, the budget is only part of the draft plan.

## Step 9: Location and Language

Recommended settings:

| Setting | Entry |
|---|---|
| Location | United States, or your target country |
| Language | English |

If your store targets Bangladesh, UAE, or another country, use that instead.

## Step 10: Final URL Expansion

Recommended for assignment draft:

`Off`

Reason: the assignment is focused on one product, so we want traffic going to the Swedish dishcloth product page.

If Google recommends turning it on, you can mention:

```text
Final URL expansion kept off for the first draft so the campaign focuses on the selected product page.
```

## Step 11: Create Asset Group

Asset group name:

```text
Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack
```

Final URL:

```text
https://yourstore.com/products/reusable-swedish-dishcloth-10-pack
```

Display paths:

```text
dishcloths
10-pack
```
