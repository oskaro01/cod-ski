## Brand Kit

**Brand name:** CleanNest Kitchen  
**Product:** Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack  
**Store type:** Eco-friendly kitchen cleaning products  
**Audience:** Home cooks, families, apartment dwellers, coffee drinkers, and practical low-waste shoppers  
**Brand personality:** Practical, warm, clean, calm, helpful, low-pressure  
**Main message:** Make everyday kitchen cleanup less wasteful and easier to keep up with.

### Visual Style

- Bright modern kitchen photography
- Warm natural morning light
- Clean white counters, light oak wood, soft neutral styling
- Reusable Swedish dishcloths in neat stacks or active use
- Realistic cellulose/cotton texture
- Simple low-waste message without overdoing leaves, recycling symbols, or fake eco badges

### Color Palette

| Use | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Sage green | `#5F8D72` |
| Background | Warm cream | `#F7F3E8` |
| Accent | Muted clay | `#C8774A` |
| Text | Soft charcoal | `#2F3430` |
| Supporting neutral | Light oak beige | `#D8B88F` |

### Font Direction

Use a clean rounded sans-serif.

- Headline font: `Poppins`, `Montserrat`, or similar
- Body font: `Inter`, `Lato`, or similar
- Text style: simple, high contrast, not crowded

### Logo Direction

If no logo file is available, use a clean text wordmark:

`CleanNest Kitchen`

Use charcoal or sage green. Keep it small and tasteful.

### Global Negative Prompt

```text

```
