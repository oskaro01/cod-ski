# 1. Instagram Content Strategy

## Platform Role

Instagram should be the main visual showroom for Your Product. The goal is to make the reusable Swedish dishcloth routine feel clean, practical, and easy to copy. Instagram content should focus on lifestyle visuals, carousel education, Reels demos, and daily Story interaction.

## Audience

Instagram should target visual buyers who care about kitchen organization, home routines, low-waste swaps, and simple cleaning habits. Key segments include apartment dwellers, home cooks, coffee drinkers, families, and practical eco-conscious shoppers.

## Posting Frequency

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Feed posts | 4-5 per week | Keep the profile active and product-focused |
| Reels | 4-5 per week | Demonstrate quick wipe-downs and kitchen routines |
| Stories | Daily, 3-5 frames | Polls, reminders, behind-the-scenes, and product links |
| Carousels | 2 per week | Educational saves and step-by-step routines |

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Instagram Angle |
|---|---|
| Educational | "How to use Swedish dishcloths" and "rinse, wring, dry" carousel posts |
| Product Use Case | Coffee rings, crumbs, sink splashes, table wipe-downs |
| Routine Building | Sunday reset, morning coffee reset, end-of-day kitchen reset |
| Engagement | Polls, this-or-that cloth colors, "what is your repeat mess?" |
| Promotional | 10-pack product reminders and sink-side swap CTA |

## Creative Direction

Use bright kitchen photography, soft natural light, clean counters, sage/cream/clay tones, and the supplied `YOUR PRODUCT` logo. Reels should be vertical, short, and safe-zone aware with the product visible early. Meta recommends Reels creative with vertical video, quality audio, and key messages kept in the safe zone.

## Hashtag Set

Use 8-12 hashtags per Instagram post:

`#YourProductHome #YourProductRoutine #SwedishDishcloths #ReusableDishcloth #KitchenCleaning #KitchenReset #LowWasteHome #EcoFriendlyKitchen #SimpleSwaps #CoffeeStation`

## Example Weekly Flow

| Day | Content |
|---|---|
| Monday | Carousel: 5 places to use a Swedish dishcloth |
| Tuesday | Reel: coffee ring cleanup in 15 seconds |
| Wednesday | Static post: paper towel swap for small messes |
| Thursday | Story poll: "Where should a fresh cloth live?" |
| Friday | Reel: dinner prep wipe-down |
| Sunday | Carousel or Story: weekly cloth rotation reset |

## KPIs

Track Reels watch time, saves, shares, profile visits, Story link taps, comments, and product-page clicks from Ecommerized.

# 2. Facebook Content Strategy

## Platform Role

Facebook should support trust, education, and community. The audience is more likely to respond to useful home tips, practical product explanations, family kitchen routines, and discussion-style posts. Facebook should also act as a steady traffic channel back to the Ecommerized product page.

## Audience

Facebook should target adults managing home cleaning routines, families, homeowners, apartment renters, and shoppers who prefer practical household products over trend-heavy content. Messaging should be clear, helpful, and slightly more explanatory than Instagram.

## Posting Frequency

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Feed posts | 4 per week | Educational tips, product benefits, and community prompts |
| Short videos/Reels | 2-3 per week | Reuse strongest Instagram/TikTok demos |
| Stories | 2-3 per week | Quick reminders, polls, and product link prompts |
| Community posts | 1-2 per week | Questions, cleaning habits, and useful home discussions |

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Facebook Angle |
|---|---|
| Educational | Explain what Swedish dishcloths are and how to care for them |
| Product Use Case | Family table reset, sink splash cleanup, fridge-handle wipe-downs |
| Routine Building | Weekly cloth rotation and Sunday kitchen reset |
| Engagement | Comment prompts and simple polls about kitchen cleaning habits |
| Promotional | Soft product reminders with "Shop the 10-pack" CTA |

## Creative Direction

Use captioned images, short videos, and simple carousel-style posts. Facebook posts can include slightly longer captions than Instagram, but the first two lines should still be clear. Use the logo in the image corner and keep product visuals realistic. Facebook Pages are designed for businesses to share updates and connect with followers, so the content should invite comments instead of only pushing sales.

## Hashtag Set

Use 3-5 hashtags per Facebook post:

`#YourProductHome #KitchenCleaning #SwedishDishcloths #KitchenReset #ReusableCleaning`

## Example Weekly Flow

| Day | Content |
|---|---|
| Monday | Educational post: what Swedish dishcloths are used for |
| Wednesday | Video: sink-side wipe-down routine |
| Friday | Engagement post: "What kitchen mess shows up every day?" |
| Saturday | Product post: why a 10-pack helps weekly rotation |
| Sunday | Routine post: Sunday reset checklist |

## KPIs

Track reach, comments, shares, post saves, link clicks, product-page visits, follower growth, and message inquiries from Facebook.

# 3. TikTok Content Strategy

## Platform Role

TikTok should be the discovery and product-demo channel. The strategy should make everyday kitchen messes feel instantly recognizable, then show the reusable dishcloth as the simple solution. TikTok content should feel native, quick, practical, and lightly personality-driven.

## Audience

TikTok should target younger home renters, apartment dwellers, first-home shoppers, students with small kitchens, coffee lovers, and viewers interested in cleaning hacks, home resets, and low-waste swaps.

## Posting Frequency

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Short videos | 5-7 per week | Primary discovery and product education |
| Trend-adapted videos | 1-2 per week | Stay culturally relevant without forcing trends |
| Comment-reply videos | 1 per week after comments start | Build trust and answer objections |
| Product demo videos | 3-4 per week | Show the cloth in action for repeat messes |

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | TikTok Angle |
|---|---|
| Educational | "You are leaving your cloth balled up" or "rinse, wring, dry" tips |
| Product Use Case | Quick before/after wipes for crumbs, coffee rings, and sink splashes |
| Routine Building | POV: setting up a sink-side reset station |
| Engagement | "Which kitchen mess are you?" and reply-to-comment videos |
| Promotional | Soft CTA videos showing the 10-pack and weekly rotation |

## Creative Direction

Use 9:16 vertical video, real kitchen movement, close-up wiping shots, voiceover, and readable text overlays. TikTok recommends TikTok-first creative that uses sound or music, vertical orientation, at least 720p resolution, safe-zone placement, and people or creators interacting with the product. Keep videos around 15-30 seconds unless the topic needs more detail.

## Hook Examples

- "Wait, you're still using paper towels for this?"
- "The coffee corner reset nobody talks about."
- "If your sink area always looks messy, try this."
- "One cloth, three kitchen reset jobs."
- "POV: you finally made your reusable swap easy to reach."

## Hashtag Set

Use 3-5 hashtags per TikTok:

`#CleanTok #KitchenTok #SwedishDishcloths #KitchenReset #LowWasteHome`

## Example Weekly Flow

| Day | Content |
|---|---|
| Monday | Demo: crumbs after breakfast |
| Tuesday | Tip: rinse, wring, dry in the open |
| Wednesday | POV: coffee corner cleanup |
| Thursday | Trend adaptation: "things in my kitchen that just make sense" |
| Friday | Demo: dinner prep wipe-down |
| Sunday | Routine: reset cloths for the week |

## KPIs

Track 3-second views, average watch time, completion rate, shares, saves, comments, profile visits, product link clicks, and repeated comment questions that can become future videos.
