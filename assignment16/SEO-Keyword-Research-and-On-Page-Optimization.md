## Assignment Overview

**Assignment task:** Complete keyword research, map 30 keywords to pages, optimize 5 product page titles/descriptions/alt text, and rewrite 3 collection page descriptions for SEO.  
**Brand:** Your Product  
**Logo tagline:** Cleaner Home, Easier Life  
**Store type:** Eco-friendly home and kitchen cleaning products  
**Primary product:** Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack  
**Primary CTA:** Shop the Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack  
**Primary product link:** `/products/reusable-swedish-dishcloth-10-pack`

## Method

This SEO plan uses keyword intent, prior assignment research, product-page relevance, and public keyword clues from marketplace/SEO sources. Exact search volume should be validated in Google Keyword Planner, Semrush, Ahrefs, or Ecommerized analytics before final publishing.

## Context Used

- `assignment10/2-Blog-Posts.md`: existing blog topics and internal-link structure.
- `assignment11/Content-Package-Doc.md`: brand voice and social/product messaging.
- `assignment13/Social-Media-Strategy-Doc.md`: Your Product brand identity and content pillars.
- `assignment14/Social-Platform-Content-Strategies.md`: channel positioning and audience.
- `assignment5/Product-Validation-Report.md`: validated product ideas for Swedish dishcloths and silicone stove gap covers.

## SEO Rules

- Give each keyword one primary target page to avoid keyword cannibalization.
- Use product pages for high-intent buying keywords.
- Use collection pages for broader category keywords.
- Use blog pages for educational or comparison keywords.
- Keep claims practical and verified. Do not claim "zero waste," "chemical-free," "guaranteed," or "best."
- Use natural language instead of keyword stuffing.
