# Video Ad Creative Brief

## Assignment

Create a product video ad for social media.

## Recommended default

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Product | Your Product Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack |
| Format | Vertical 9:16 |
| Canvas | 1080 x 1920 |
| Length | 60 to 75 seconds |
| Platforms | TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels |
| Audience | Home cooks, apartment dwellers, families, coffee drinkers, and practical low-waste shoppers |
| Objective | Product awareness and product-page visits |
| CTA | Shop the Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack |
| Tone | Practical, warm, clean, low-pressure |

The 15-second version can be exported later as a short social cutdown. The main assignment version should use the longer storyboard in Video-Ad-60-Second-Script-and-Storyboard.md.

## Why this product

This product is a strong fit for a short ad because the problem is easy to recognize and the use is easy to demonstrate:

- Crumbs on the counter
- Coffee rings
- Sink splashes
- Table wipe-downs
- A simple wet, wring, wipe, rinse, and dry routine

The 10-pack also gives the video a practical reason to buy: keeping fresh cloths ready for rotation.

## Core message

Small kitchen messes can fit into a simple reusable routine.

## Story

1. Show a familiar small kitchen mess.
2. Introduce the reusable dishcloth as the practical next step.
3. Show the simple routine.
4. End with the product, logo, and CTA.

## Creative direction

- Use close-up kitchen footage with hands and real movement.
- Keep the product visible before the final two seconds if possible.
- Use clean sage, cream, clay, and dark blue accents from the Your Product brand.
- Keep captions in the upper or middle safe area, away from the bottom interface controls.
- Use large, high-contrast text that can be read with sound off.
- Use quick cuts only when they help the viewer follow the routine.
- Use a soft product reveal at the end rather than a crowded collage.

## Claim safety

Safe wording:

- Reusable
- Everyday kitchen wipe-downs
- Practical paper towel alternative
- Simple kitchen reset
- Keep fresh cloths ready
- Wet, wring, wipe, rinse, dry

Avoid unless verified:

- Zero waste
- Chemical-free
- Antibacterial
- Germ-free
- Best
- Guaranteed
- Fake review or fake customer result
- Fake discount or urgency

## Optional original footage

Original phone footage is better when the real product is available:

1. Place the cloth beside a sink.
2. Add a few crumbs or a coffee ring to a counter.
3. Film a hand wetting and wringing the cloth.
4. Film one clean wipe across the mess.
5. Film the cloth rinsing and drying in an open spot.
6. Film the 10-pack and logo for the final frame.

If stock footage shows a generic cloth, do not present it as a close-up of the exact Your Product item. Use it as illustrative routine footage and show the Your Product visual separately.

## Music direction

Use a light, clean, rhythmic instrumental track. For a paid social ad, use music cleared for the intended platform or a license that expressly covers paid advertising. Keep music below any voiceover and make sure the message still works when muted.
