# Video Ad Project: Your Product

## Project

Create a 60 to 75 second vertical video ad for the Your Product Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack.

## Recommended submission

1. Final vertical MP4 video
2. Script and storyboard
3. Screenshot of the editing timeline or export settings
4. Asset and license notes for every stock clip, image, music track, and logo used

## Important footage note

Do not download a random video from Google Images or YouTube and assume it is reusable. Use your own footage or stock footage with a license that permits the intended use. Keep the original URL and a screenshot or download record with your submission.

The candidate Pexels clips in the creative brief are suggestions. Recheck the specific asset page and license when downloading.

## Who does what

The assistant handles the script, scene order, captions, voiceover plan, music guidance, timing, editing, and final export after the source clips are available.

The student needs to provide either original kitchen clips or downloaded stock clips. Put them in assignment21/assets. Three to five clips are enough because the final video also uses the product visual, logo, text cards, and end card.

The 15-second storyboard is an optional short version. Use the 60 to 75 second storyboard for the main assignment.
