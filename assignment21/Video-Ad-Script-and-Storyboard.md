## Text placement

- Keep the first hook in the upper-middle area.
- Keep routine captions near the center but away from the hand and cloth.
- Keep the CTA above the bottom platform controls.
- Use one highlight color for REUSABLE or SHOP NOW.
- Avoid placing text over detailed crumbs, fingers, or the product logo.
