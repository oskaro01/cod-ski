# 60 to 75 Second Video Ad Script and Storyboard

## Main assignment version

Format: Vertical 9:16  
Target length: 60 to 75 seconds  
Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts  
Audio: Voiceover plus low background music; the captions must make sense without sound  
Timing note: These are target edit timings for assembly after the source clips are available.

## Voiceover script

Every kitchen has the little messes that happen between meals.

Crumbs after breakfast. A coffee ring on the counter. Water splashes around the sink. Small jobs, but they show up every day.

That is where a simple reusable routine can help.

Meet the Your Product Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack. Keep a cloth near the sink, wet it, wring it out, and wipe the mess. Rinse it after use, then let it dry in an open spot.

Use one for the counter, one for the table, one for the sink area, and keep fresh cloths ready for the next reset. The 10-pack makes rotation easier through a busy week.

There is no complicated system. Just a reusable cloth ready when everyday kitchen cleanup happens.

Your Product. Cleaner Home, Easier Life.

Shop the Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack.

## Audio plan

### Voiceover

Use a clear, friendly, conversational voice. The delivery should feel helpful rather than aggressive. The voiceover should be recorded or generated after the final script is approved.

### Music

Choose a clean, light instrumental track. Use platform-cleared music or a license that explicitly covers commercial advertising. Keep the music below the voice and lower it further during important product instructions.

### Sound effects

Optional effects:

- Quiet cup or counter sound at the opening
- Soft water sound during wet and rinse scenes
- One gentle impact when the product visual appears

Do not add an effect to every cut.

## Material needed before final assembly

- Three to five kitchen clips, either original or licensed stock
- The supplied product visual
- The supplied logo
- Voiceover audio, if used
- Licensed music, if used

The clips do not need to be longer than the scene durations. They can be trimmed, reframed, slowed slightly, or used as short inserts. The final length comes from combining clips with product visuals, text cards, the voiceover, and the end card.
