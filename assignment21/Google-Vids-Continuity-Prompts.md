## What to do

Create the ad as a sequence of short scenes. Do not ask the AI to invent one long one-minute shot.

Recommended structure:

- 8 short generated scenes
- About 7 to 8 seconds per scene
- One 4 to 6 second end card
- Total length: about 60 to 70 seconds
- Portrait format: 9:16

Google Vids can create a draft outline from a prompt and can also generate individual clips from detailed shot descriptions. Edit the scene prompts after the outline appears. Keep one voiceover and one music track across the whole timeline.

### How to use the interface

- Use `Create` or `Help me create` for the first project outline and for separate short scenes.
- Use `Insert` to place a generated clip into the timeline before leaving the generation view.
- Use `Extend` only when you want the selected generated clip to continue directly from its final frame. Start the prompt with `Continue directly from the final frame` and describe the next action.
- Use separate scene prompts when you want a clean cut between different actions, such as presenter to close-up product demonstration.
- Use `Recreate` only when the scene itself is wrong. It may change the woman's face, product appearance, or kitchen.
- Do not depend on `Upscale` for the first draft. A consistent story and clean edit matter more than upscaling one clip.
