# Video Ad Scene Prompts

## How to use these prompts

Use one prompt per generated clip. Set the video tool to:

- Vertical 9:16
- 5 to 8 seconds per clip
- Realistic product-commercial style
- Warm natural kitchen light
- No generated text or logos
- No invented product claims

Use the same anchor description in every prompt:

Bright practical home kitchen, light wood counter, cream and sage color palette, soft natural daylight, calm everyday home atmosphere, realistic hands, clean commercial photography, vertical 9:16.

If the video tool accepts a reference image, provide the Your Product product visual for the product scenes. Generated footage may change the exact cloth design, so use the supplied product visual for the final product end card.

Keep the same kitchen, counter color, lighting direction, cloth material, hand appearance, and calm commercial tone across every scene. Do not place readable words inside the generated footage. Add all captions and the real Your Product logo in the editor.
