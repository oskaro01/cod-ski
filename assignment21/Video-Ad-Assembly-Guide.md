# Video Ad Assembly Guide

## Editing workflow

This workflow works in CapCut, Canva, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or another timeline editor.

### 1. Create the project

Use:

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Duration: 60 to 75 seconds
- Frame rate: match the downloaded clips; 30 fps is a practical default for a new phone edit
- Export: MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio

### 2. Import the assets

Import:

- The selected stock clips or your own phone footage
- assignment13/assets/logo_your_prod.png
- assignment13/assets/swedish-dishcloth-product-image.png
- The script from Video-Ad-Script-and-Storyboard.md
- Licensed music for the intended platform

### 3. Build the sequence

1. Start with the clearest mess or coffee-ring shot.
2. Establish the everyday kitchen problem.
3. Introduce the product visual and brand.
4. Show wet and wring.
5. Show one satisfying wipe.
6. Show several real use cases.
7. Explain the 10-pack rotation.
8. Show rinse and open drying.
9. Finish with the Your Product logo, product visual, tagline, and CTA.

Do not use the existing 03-promotional-ad.mp4 from the Ecommerized Video Kit for this project. That file promotes Ecommerized software, not the Swedish dishcloth product.

### 4. Add captions

Add the phrase captions from the storyboard. Use:

- A bold, readable sans-serif font
- White text with a dark outline or semi-transparent background
- Short phrases instead of full paragraphs
- One consistent accent color
- At least enough screen time to read each phrase comfortably

### 5. Add music and voice

- Keep the voiceover clear if used.
- Lower music under speech.
- Add one subtle whoosh or impact only at the opening or product reveal.
- Do not rely on music to communicate the product message.
- Confirm the music license permits commercial or paid social use.

### 6. Add the end card

Use the supplied logo and product image. Keep the final frame clean:

YOUR PRODUCT
CLEANER HOME, EASIER LIFE
Reusable Swedish Dishcloth 10-Pack
SHOP NOW
