## Calculator Assumptions

| Input | Assumption |
|---|---:|
| Pieces per retail pack | 10 |
| Target selling price | $14.99 |
| Customs / duty planning rate | 12.8% |
| Duty basis | Product cost only |
| Order size | 1,000 retail packs |
| Fulfillment, payment fees, returns, and ad spend | Not included in landed cost |

Customs note: The calculator uses HTS 6307.10 planning logic for floorcloths, dishcloths, dusters, and similar cleaning cloths. The 12.8% estimate combines an assumed 5.3% base duty with an estimated 7.5% China-origin additional duty. Final classification and duty should be confirmed with a customs broker before ordering.

## Formula Used

```text
Product cost per pack = selected unit price per piece x 10 pieces
Customs per pack = product cost per pack x customs rate
Total landed cost per pack = product cost per pack + shipping per pack + customs per pack + packaging per pack
Gross profit before fulfillment + ads = target selling price - total landed cost per pack
Margin before fulfillment + ads = gross profit / target selling price
```

## Best Option

Best supplier: Ningbo Heyuan Textile Product Co., Ltd.

- Lowest total landed cost per retail pack: $4.73
