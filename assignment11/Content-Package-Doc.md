## Brand Voice Guide

CleanNest Kitchen sounds practical, warm, clear, and gently encouraging. The brand helps customers make low-waste kitchen swaps without guilt, pressure, or unrealistic promises.

**Voice pillars:**

- **Practical:** Focus on real daily messes: counters, coffee rings, sink splashes, crumbs, and table wipe-downs.
- **Warm:** Speak like a helpful kitchen friend, not a lecture.
- **Low-pressure:** Encourage simple swaps without shaming paper towel use.
- **Clear:** Use plain words, short sentences, and specific benefits.
- **Trustworthy:** Avoid unsupported claims, fake urgency, or exaggerated environmental promises.

**Words to use:** reusable, simple swap, daily wipe-downs, kitchen reset, easy rotation, less wasteful, practical, sink-side, fresh cloth, everyday messes.

**Words to avoid:** miracle, guaranteed, chemical-free unless verified, zero waste unless proven, best ever, life-changing, guilt-free as a manipulative claim.

## Email 2: Education and Habit Building

**Timing:** 1 day after signup  
**Goal:** Teach the customer how to use Swedish dishcloths and make the product feel easy to adopt.  
**Subject line:** How to use Swedish dishcloths without overthinking it  
**Preheader:** A simple rotation system for daily kitchen messes.

**Email body:**

Hi there,

Reusable products work best when they are easy to use. That is why Swedish dishcloths are one of our favorite starter swaps for the kitchen.

Here is the easiest way to use them:

1. Wet the cloth.
2. Wring it out.
3. Wipe counters, tables, sink areas, or small spills.
4. Rinse after use.
5. Let it air dry in an open spot.

That is it.

The real trick is rotation. A single cloth can get overworked quickly, especially in a busy kitchen. With a 10-pack, you can keep fresh cloths ready and separate them by task:

- One for counters
- One for the dining table
- One for sink-area wipe-downs
- One backup for surprise spills

Move used cloths to a small laundry bin or cleaning basket when they need a refresh. Then place a clean cloth back near the sink so the reusable option is always easy to grab.

You do not have to stop using paper towels completely. Some messes may still call for a disposable option. But for everyday crumbs, water rings, and quick surface resets, a Swedish dishcloth can do the job without making your routine harder.

Small habit. Real use. Cleaner kitchen rhythm.
