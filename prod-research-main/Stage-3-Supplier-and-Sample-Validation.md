# Stage 3: Supplier and Sample Validation

Use this file only after a product passes Stage 2 and receives a **SAMPLE** decision.

Stage 3 answers one practical question:

> Does the real product, supplier, fulfilment plan, content potential, and updated economics justify a controlled test?

Do not recommend inventory from supplier photos, marketplace reviews, or a high research score alone.

## Stage 3 entry gate

Complete this checklist before contacting suppliers:

- [ ] Stage 2 individual product export is complete.
- [ ] Stage 2 detailed evidence report is complete.
- [ ] No critical safety or compliance gate has failed.
- [ ] The product has a plausible base-case contribution after variable costs.
- [ ] The target country, channel, fulfilment model, and test budget are known.
- [ ] The exact product variant or specification to request is defined.
- [ ] The sample pass/fail criteria are written before the sample arrives.

If any critical item is missing, mark the product **RESEARCH MORE** instead of moving forward blindly.

## 3. Supplier outreach message

Adapt this message to the product and target market:

```text
Hello,

I am evaluating your [exact product name and variant] for a small test in [target GCC country]. Please confirm the following for the exact model shown in this product link:

1. Sample price and sample shipping cost to [city, country].
2. Unit price for [quantity], [quantity], and [quantity] units.
3. MOQ for the standard product and for custom packaging.
4. Product materials, dimensions, weight, colour options, and included accessories.
5. Production time, handling time, and shipping methods.
6. Shipping Incoterm: DDP, DAP, or another arrangement.
7. Estimated delivery time and whether tracking is provided.
8. Available product safety, testing, compliance, and labelling documents.
9. Defect, replacement, warranty, and refund policy.
10. Packaging options, logo printing, barcode, inserts, and packaging cost.
11. Can you provide photos or video of the exact product before shipment?
12. Can you support repeat orders if the test performs well?

Please send a written quotation with the quote validity period. Thank you.
```

### Product-specific tests

Add tests for the product's actual risk. Examples:

- Electronics: charging, battery safety, heat, pairing, app failure, and reset behavior.
- Beauty or skin-contact products: ingredients, expiry, packaging, claims, irritation risk, and required documentation.
- Household products: repeated use, cleaning, leaks, odour, staining, and material durability.
- Clothing or accessories: sizing, colour accuracy, stitching, comfort, and wash or wear behavior.
- Fitness products: load, stability, user safety, instructions, and misuse risk.
- Food-contact products: material documentation, cleaning, heat, odour, and labelling.

### Golden sample and production quality control

If the sample passes, retain a dated and photographed **golden sample** as the reference for future production.

Before pilot-order approval, confirm:

- Golden sample approval.
- Inspection procedure and responsible party.
- Critical, major, and minor defect definitions.
- Acceptable defect limits and rejection rule.
- Written replacement or refund conditions.
- Supplier production-quality obligations.

Before production shipment, confirm:

- The actual production batch was inspected.
- The inspection report was reviewed.
- The batch matches the golden sample.
- Defect limits were verified.
- Packaging and labelling were inspected.
- Shipment release was approved.

The production batch must match the approved golden sample in the attributes that affect customer experience, safety, function, and UGC claims.

## 6. Updated unit economics

Replace Stage 2 estimates with supplier and sample evidence.

```text
Calculation basis = per unit or per customer order; state which one is used.

If the customer-facing price includes VAT:
  Net revenue = gross customer price / (1 + VAT rate)
  Output VAT = gross customer price - net revenue

If the customer-facing price excludes VAT:
  Calculate the customer-facing gross price separately.

Production landed product cost = bulk unit cost
  + production freight allocation
  + non-recoverable duty/customs
  + non-recoverable import VAT
  + brokerage/clearance
  + production inspection allocation
  + per-unit packaging cost
  + other applicable sourcing costs

Contribution before advertising = net revenue
  - landed product cost
  - platform commission
  - payment or BNPL fee
  - fulfilment fee
  - buyer or seller shipping cost
  - expected return/refund cost
  - customer support or handling cost
  - expected failed-delivery and reverse-logistics cost
  - payment collection fees

Contribution after advertising = contribution before advertising - CAC

Break-even CAC = contribution before advertising

Break-even ROAS per order = advertising-platform-reported conversion revenue per order / break-even CAC per order

Break-even ROAS for an aggregate campaign = total platform-reported conversion revenue for the same order cohort / total contribution before advertising for that same order cohort

Actual campaign ROAS = total platform-reported conversion revenue / actual advertising spend
```

### VAT and revenue basis

Record the target country's VAT rate and the seller's VAT-registration status. Show the customer-facing price including VAT separately from revenue used for contribution analysis.

- Use revenue excluding output VAT when the seller must collect VAT.
- Treat recoverable input VAT as a cash-flow item rather than a permanent cost when legally recoverable.
- Include non-recoverable import VAT, customs duty, brokerage, and other non-recoverable taxes where applicable.
- Confirm whether platform, payment, BNPL, and advertising fees are calculated on VAT-inclusive or VAT-exclusive amounts.
- Use the same revenue basis for break-even CAC and break-even ROAS.

If VAT status or fee basis is unknown, show the assumption and a sensitivity case.

For break-even ROAS, state whether the advertising platform reports conversion revenue including or excluding VAT, shipping, discounts, refunds, and other adjustments. Use the same reported-revenue definition in the ROAS formula and the pilot result.

For order-level economics, multiply product and per-unit packaging costs by the actual quantity in the order. Account for bundles and multipacks, use average order value when appropriate, and calculate CAC per acquired order. Compare order-level CAC with order-level contribution before advertising. Avoid double-counting any landed-cost component.

Calculate one expected failed-delivery and reverse-logistics cost per order using the probability of failure and the applicable return-to-origin, reverse-shipping, refused-delivery, and handling charges. Include separate components only when they represent genuinely different costs. Keep payment collection fees separate.

A failed critical gate overrides the numerical score. A pending gate means the product is not ready for inventory.

The QC plan and supplier obligations must PASS before pilot-order approval. The production-batch inspection and shipment-release gate may remain PENDING at that point, but it must PASS before the production shipment is released.

Do not authorize supplier payment or a controlled pilot order while a critical supplier-identity or payment-beneficiary mismatch remains unresolved. Use a protected payment method where practical. If it is unavailable, document the additional transaction risk in the final decision.

## 9. Final decision

Choose one:

- **GO FOR CONTROLLED PILOT:** the sample, documentation, fulfilment, UGC, supplier identity, payment safety, QC plan, documented supplier production-quality obligations, and base-case economics pass. This approves a limited pilot order subject to successful pre-shipment inspection and shipment release; it does not approve unrestricted inventory purchasing.
- **FIX AND RETEST:** a specific fix or additional document could change the result; name the owner and deadline.
- **NO-GO:** a critical quality, compliance, fulfilment, or economic problem makes the current offer unsuitable.
