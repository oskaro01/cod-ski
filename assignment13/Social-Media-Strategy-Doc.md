## Strategy Goal

The goal for August is to build awareness and product confidence for Your Product's reusable Swedish dishcloth 10-pack. The content focuses on everyday kitchen messes, simple low-waste habits, and practical sink-side routines. The tone stays warm and low-pressure, avoiding exaggerated eco claims or fake urgency.

## Target Audience

| Segment | Why They Matter | Content Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Home cooks | They clean counters, prep areas, and tables throughout the day. | Clean-as-you-go tips and dinner prep resets |
| Apartment dwellers | They need compact, easy-to-store cleaning tools. | Small-space kitchen routines |
| Families | They deal with repeat messes from meals and snacks. | Table wipe-downs, crumbs, and weekly rotation |
| Coffee drinkers | Coffee rings and small drips create daily wipe-down moments. | Coffee corner cleanup |
| Low-waste shoppers | They want practical swaps without guilt or perfection pressure. | Paper towel alternative for everyday messes |

## Claim Safety Rules

- Do not claim zero waste, chemical-free, germ-free, antibacterial, guaranteed, certified, or best unless verified.
- Do not invent reviews, discounts, certifications, awards, or scarcity.
- Keep the core promise practical: reusable cloths for everyday kitchen wipe-downs.
- Use low-pressure wording such as "use fewer paper towels for everyday messes" instead of absolute replacement claims.
