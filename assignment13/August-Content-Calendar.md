## Brand Voice and Claim Rules

Your Product should sound practical, warm, calm, clear, and low-pressure. The content should help customers make simple low-waste kitchen swaps without guilt or exaggerated promises.

**Use these phrases often:** reusable, simple swap, daily wipe-downs, kitchen reset, easy rotation, less wasteful, practical, sink-side, fresh cloth, everyday messes.

**Avoid these claims unless verified:** zero waste, chemical-free, guaranteed, best ever, germ-free, antibacterial, saves hundreds, certified compostable, or any fake reviews and ratings.
