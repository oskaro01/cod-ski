> Goal: Find the best **Camera (TPP No-Scope)** and **ADS** sensitivity for hitting the most headshots with hipfire / no-scope gameplay.

## 1. Your intuition was RIGHT: lower ADS = more headshots ✅

You said *"when I lower ADS the more headshots hit"* — that's a real, well-known effect in PUBG Mobile, not a fluke. Here's why:

- **Precision beats speed.** Headshots need pinpoint crosshair placement. At high ADS sensitivity, a tiny thumb drag whips the crosshair *past* the enemy's head (overshooting). Lower ADS = smaller, cleaner micro-adjustments right onto the skull.
- **Recoil control.** Lower ADS means your recoil-compensation drag (pulling down on an M416/AKM) translates into smoother, tighter spray patterns — keeping bullets clustered around the upper chest/head zone instead of scattering.
- **The "ADS Cascade" rule.** Pros always run ADS sensitivity that *decreases* as magnification increases (Red Dot ~55% → 8x ~12%). This builds consistent muscle memory: low-zoom adjustments need bigger swipes and more control.

### The catch — don't go TOO low
Lower ADS helps *consistency*, but if you go too low you'll "feel slow" and struggle to flick onto moving targets in close fights. The headshot-focused sweet spot is **~50–70% for Red Dot/Holo ADS**, and **~95–110% for TPP No-Scope ADS**.

---

## 2. Camera (TPP No-Scope) — what to use

The **Camera sensitivity** controls your free-look / hipfire turn speed when you're NOT aiming. For no-scope gameplay:

- **95–100%** → controlled, precise, great for tracking head height on a target
- **100–120%** → the balanced pro zone (recommended starting point)
- **120–130%** → aggressive flicks and close-range jiggle movement (harder to keep on heads)

**Recommendation:** start at **Camera TPP No-Scope = 105%** and tweak ±5% until your crosshair lands on heads naturally without overshooting.

## 5. Extra tips for headshot consistency

1. **ADS in, don't hip-spray.** Even "no-scope" fights hit more heads if you quick-ADS (tap aim) rather than full hipfire.
2. **Aim at neck/upper-chest height** when tracking — recoil naturally climbs into the head.
3. **Test the ladder:** go to Training Grounds, pick the M416, and test 3 values (e.g. 45% / 55% / 65% ADS Red Dot) against moving bots. Pick the one where your *first shot* lands on heads most often.
4. **Gyroscope players:** lower both sliders another ~10% — gyro gives you the fine control back, so you can go even lower on touch sensitivity for cleaner head taps.
